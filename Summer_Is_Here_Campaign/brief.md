## 1. Research Summary

**Seasonal context (known):**
- June–August is UAE **low** season for inbound tourism, **high** season for resident staycations. The buying audience is residents, not tourists.
- Heat is the story. Indoor and water-based experiences outperform outdoor ones in August.
- Schools reopen **late August**, which creates a genuine, non-manufactured deadline.
- Back to School campaigns are about to dominate every competitor inbox.

**Strategic implication:** this is a *last-chance* campaign, not a *season-opener*. The urgency is real — use it rather than inventing scarcity.
